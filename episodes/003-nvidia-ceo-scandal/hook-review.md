## Problems
1. No stakes in the first 15 s: the viewer does not know why to care until about 44–50 s.
2. The first strong image arrives at 19 s; the first second is nearly empty.
3. The first face (the main character) comes at 44 s.
4. Several scenes started empty for up to 3.5 s because the main picture was timed to a late word (23 scenes in the whole video). FIXED in code (rule R-EMPTY: first picture within 0.25 s); needs the next render.

## Plan (visual only, no re-recording of the approved voice)
- **H1. Stakes strip, 0:00–0:50.** A slim ribbon at the top: "NVIDIA CEO · OUT NOV 12 · BACK NOV 22". The viewer knows from second 1 what the story is. (cost: small)
- **H2. Cold open (0–5 s).** Replace the dark title + passive map with: crates slam in with the SEALED stamp at 0.2 s (impact), "LATE 2024 · MALAYSIA" ribbon, then the map pin. First strong image at 0.2 s, not 19 s.
- **H3. Make 5–19 s move.** The official walks in from the left and stops at the door; the door opens on the crates; camera pushes in slowly. No static hold longer than 4 s.
- **H4. Pin Jensen earlier.** On the corkboard (33–44 s) put Jensen as the large centre card from the start, strings radiating to the others, so the star is visible at 33 s, not 44 s.
- **H5. Pattern interrupts.** One small hit every 6–8 s in the first minute (stamp, slide, zoom) so there is never a 10 s flat stretch.

## Expected effect
Better retention in the first 30 s (the part YouTube weighs most). I cannot give a number: measure "average view duration" and the "watch time at 0:30" graph in YouTube Studio for the first 48 hours and compare with the next episode.
