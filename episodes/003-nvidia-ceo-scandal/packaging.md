## Rules we followed (and why)
- Max 4 big words, one idea per thumbnail, readable at phone size.
- Allegations stay questions or are marked UNPROVEN. Never state "spy" as a fact. This also protects the channel from strikes and keeps the video balanced.
- Titles avoid "exposed", "caught" and "guilty".
