## Conflicts and traps to avoid
- Tom's Hardware snippet says the suspension "expires on Nov 27, 2026"; S&P, Benchmark and Global Trade Alert agree on Jan 10, 2027 after a Nov 9 notice. Use Jan 10.
- "White House target to block all Chinese rare earth imports in Jan 2027" appeared in one analysis. It is really the DFARS defense-procurement restriction on Jan 1, 2027. The script says "a Pentagon rule" and "defense contractors".
- One result says Bessent announced the extension "as Xi arrived in the US" (a different date). Not used.
- US-China summit sequence differs across sources (Busan Oct 2025, Beijing May 2026, Xi at the White House Sept 2026, Shenzhen Nov 2026). The script uses Busan, Beijing and Shenzhen only.
- Announcement numbers differ (58, 61, 62, 70). Do not say a number aloud.

## Never say
- That China "banned" rare earth exports to the US. It licenses them.
- That the 0.1% rule is in force. It is suspended.
- That any company or person did something unlawful. Nothing in this script alleges that; no allegation words are used.
