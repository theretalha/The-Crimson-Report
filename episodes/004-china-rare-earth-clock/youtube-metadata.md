## Description (paste as is)
China has extended a pause on new rare earth export controls until January 10, 2027. Nine days earlier, on January 1, a US defense rule on magnets takes effect, and the supply chain to replace Chinese magnets does not exist yet. Why do two governments circle the same nine days? Here is how a small grey magnet can stop a car factory, why China controls the middle of the supply chain, what the customs data shows, and three ways January 10 could end.

Every figure in this video is a reported number from a named source. Some are single-outlet estimates and are marked as such on screen. Nothing here accuses a company or a person of wrongdoing.
