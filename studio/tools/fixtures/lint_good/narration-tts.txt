THE CRIMSON FORECAST - LINT FIXTURE (not a real episode)

[CHAPTER 0 - THE STRAIT]
Three destroyers entered the strait just before dawn, lights off.
A tanker sat anchored near the shore, and its engines were off. <short pause>
Nobody on board answered the radio, and nobody came out on deck.
<long pause> Reportedly, the captain had sold the cargo to a Chinese firm, according to Reuters.
So why did the destroyers stop? We'll get to that.
[CHAPTER 1 - THE ORDER]
A man in a grey coat handed the captain a sealed envelope at the pier.
He didn't open it, he put it in his pocket and walked down the pier.
The envelope held one page, and it named a second tanker and a second port.
<short pause> That page is why a tanker isn't home today.
