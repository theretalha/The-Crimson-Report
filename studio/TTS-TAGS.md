# Google AI Studio TTS (Gemini 3.8): expression tags — official list from the owner (Nov 26, 2026)

Rule: when writing narration for the owner to paste into Google AI Studio, add expression with these tags.
Do NOT fake pauses or emotion with extra dots ("....") or other punctuation tricks. Paste the tag inside the text, where the sound should happen.

**Pauses & breathing:** `<short pause>` `<long pause>` `<breath>` `<heavy breath>` `<exhales>` `<gasp>` `<pant>`
**Laughter & positive:** `<laugh>` `<laughter>` `<giggle>` `<cackle>` `<snicker>` `<snort>` `<guffaw>` `<cheer>` `<applause>`
**Reactions & vocal bursts:** `<shout>` `<scream>` `<shriek>` `<yell>` `<sigh>` `<sighs>` `<yawn>` `<sneeze>` `<groan>` `<grunt>` `<cough>` `<throat-clearing>` `<tsk>` `<hiss>` `<tut>` `<argh>` `<growl>` `<grr>`
**Distress & soft tones:** `<cry>` `<sob>` `<whimper>` `<whispers>` `<whispering>`

## How to use them on this channel
- Our narrator is a calm, serious investigative voice: use mostly `<short pause>`, `<long pause>`, `<breath>`, `<sighs>`, `<whispers>`, and rarely `<tsk>` or `<gasp>`.
- Put `<long pause>` before a reveal or twist, `<short pause>` after a key number or date, `<whispers>` for "nobody can say for sure" lines.
- Avoid laughter, cheering, shouting or crying on news-documentary topics (it breaks the serious tone and can sound gossipy). Use them only if the owner asks for a lighter episode.
- Max about one tag per 2–3 sentences; too many sounds robotic.
